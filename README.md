make.fun entry for **Weirdest Job Title: Blind Judge Showdown** — one job title, 60 characters at most, nothing else.

## The title

> Airplane Seat Recline Permission Granter

40 characters. A real-sounding airline job with one hilariously specific twist: somebody has to grant permission before the seat in front of you reclines. Chosen as the best of 35 measured candidates (24/25 on weird / funny / real-sounding / specific twist / tight) after a full census of all 3,272 field titles — every keyword in the line is a zero-hit lane (recline 0, permission 0, granter 0), steering clear of the saturated senior / professional / apologizer / chief / mourner lanes.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the title to `/output/entry.txt` — UTF-8, no trailing newline, 40 bytes. Deterministic: two runs produce byte-identical output.
